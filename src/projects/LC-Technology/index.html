Dublin Airport Rail System - Leaving Cert Technology

A model rail system promoting sustainable transport between Dublin City and the Airport.



When I was 17 years old, I built a model rail system promoting sustainable transport as part of my Leaving Certificate (Secondary School). Still today, I am proud of this project. It was very cool, and foundational for developing my love and skills as an engineer. Upon designing the project, I had three clear features i wanted to implement:

  • A moving carriage.
  • A timed stop system.
  • A display showing E.T.A. of the train.

These are my favourite features; as they are electronic engineering focused. I used my skills in circuit design, soldering and programming to build a system allowing all of these features to be implemented cleanly.

A series of other cool features were implemented to my project, I used my CAD skills to design and laser cut buildings arround the train station, as well as rail platforms. I 3D printed steps, and cut the front and roof of the train by hand - making it out of sheet aluminium. I also implemented an LED system, which acted as street lights in the different rail areas.

This project went on to achieve several accolades:

  • H1 in the Leaving Certificate (Highest Grade).
  • 1st place in ETTA regional project competition.
  • Qualified for ETTA national project competition.
